# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
People in Ponta Grossa (PR) who want to start or keep playing Beach Tennis, Padel or Futevôlei. Two groups read the same page: beginners who have never held a racket and need to feel welcome asking a first question, and people who already play and want a place to keep improving, play regularly and meet others. Most arrive on a phone, from Instagram or a shared WhatsApp link.

## Product Purpose
A single landing page whose main job is to start a WhatsApp conversation about classes (turmas, horários, matrícula). Supporting arguments: four covered sand courts, two Padel courts, free parking, full changing rooms, a bar with tables, and recurring tournaments. Success = qualified class inquiries, with the chosen sport already named in the message.

## Brand Personality
Active, welcoming, confident. Social without being a party brand; organized without being a corporate gym. Copy is plain Brazilian Portuguese, short sentences, no marketing inflation.

## Anti-references
- Generic gym / CrossFit templates (black + red, "no pain no gain").
- Competitive academy tone that scares beginners.
- Court-booking platforms and SaaS-style card grids.
- Three identical icon cards, stat counters, fake testimonials, invented schedules or prices.
- Neon, glassmorphism, gradient text, glow.

## Design Principles
1. The photographs are real and they carry the page. Never replace them with decoration.
2. Classes first. Every section either builds the case for a class or leads to asking about one.
3. Say only what is verified. Missing business facts are flagged, never invented.
4. Light and dark chapters, like the reference: quiet editorial pages between cinematic ones.
5. Mobile is composed, not shrunk.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible focus states, keyboard reachable CTAs, descriptive alt text, prefers-reduced-motion honored, content visible without JavaScript.
